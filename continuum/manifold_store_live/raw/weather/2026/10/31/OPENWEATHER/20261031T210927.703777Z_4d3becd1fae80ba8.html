Company

OpenWeather is a London-based weather intelligence company serving enterprises, developers, businesses, and researchers worldwide. Through its developer business, the company provides global weather data and forecasting services via fast, reliable APIs, delivering historical, current, and forecast weather data for any location worldwide. Combining advanced meteorology, AI-enhanced forecasting models, and scalable data infrastructure, OpenWeather helps customers integrate weather intelligence into applications, services, and operational workflows.

One Call API®

  • One Call API

    All-in-One Weather Data, One Simple Call

    Access real-time weather, hourly & daily forecasts, national alerts, and historical data — through a unified timeline-based API. Whether you’re building apps, analytics, AI workflows, dashboards, and enterprise weather solutions, One Call API is your all-purpose solution. The subscription provides access to One Call API endpoint version 4.0 and endpoint version 3.0

    One Call API, endpoint version 4.0

    One Call API, endpoint version 3.0

    • Current Conditions

      Real-time weather conditions including temperature, humidity, wind, pressure, clouds, and more.

    • 1-Minute Forecast

      Minute-by-minute precipitation forecast for the next 60 minutes.

    • 15-Minute Forecast Timeline

      High-resolution 15-minute forecast timeline for the next 48 hours powered by the OWHL™ hyper-local model.

    • Hourly Weather Timeline

      Explore 47+ years+ years of hourly historical weather data and up to 48 hours of forecast records through a unified weather timeline endpoint.

    • Daily Weather Timeline

      Access long-range daily weather timelines combining 47+ years+ years of historical archive with forecasts up to 1.5 years ahead.

    • National Weather Alerts

      Government-issued weather alerts and detailed metadata from official national meteorological agencies (e.g. NOAA, EUMETNET)
