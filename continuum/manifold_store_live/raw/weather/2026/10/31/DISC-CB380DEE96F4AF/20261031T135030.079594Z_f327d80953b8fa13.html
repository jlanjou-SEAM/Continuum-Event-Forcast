The work is never the problem. The leftovers are.

You publish, you check the basics, you move on. A backlog builds up behind you, though, and none of it is ever urgent enough to schedule.

  • You moved a page a while back. Something out there still links to the old URL.

  • Hundreds of older posts have no meta description. Writing them by hand isn’t going to happen.
  • Some posts have nothing linking to them. Finding out which ones is the hard part.
  • It’s late, the fix isn’t obvious, and there’s nobody to ask.

Yoast SEO Premium takes each of these off your list. If you already use Yoast SEO, this is the part that stops being manual.

What stops being manual

Yoast suggests relevant pages to link to as you write. The orphaned content workout shows which posts have nothing pointing at them, so readers get a clearer route through your site.

Questions & answers

How many sites does one subscription cover?

One subscription covers one site or domain. You can buy more than one subscription if you run several sites, and you choose how many at checkout. Bulk purchases qualify for a discount.

What’s included in Yoast SEO Premium?

The plan includes the Yoast SEO Premium plugin, plus the Local SEO, News SEO, and Video SEO plugins. You also get one seat for the Yoast SEO Google Docs add-on, full access to Yoast SEO Academy, and 24/7 support.

What if I have more than one website?

Yoast SEO Premium subscription can be used on 1 website or domain. You can use Yoast SEO Premium on multiple websites by purchasing more than one subscription. You can choose how many sites you need it for on the check-out page. If you’re buying in bulk, you also get a discount.

How to install and activate Yoast SEO Premium?

If you have any trouble or you’re simply not sure how to get started, check out our Yoast SEO Premium installation manual.

Can I transfer my Yoast SEO Premium subscription from one site to another?

Yes! You can easily add and remove sites in MyYoast, we have a step-by-step guide that outlines how to connect a Yoast SEO subscription.

Can I use Yoast SEO Premium features in different languages?

The analysis works in more than 20 languages. Check the language availability list for the current set.

Can I use Yoast SEO Premium on my staging site?

You can use your Yoast SEO Premium on your staging site. Simply add both your live and staging site in MyYoast and transfer subscription(s) between the live and development site. You can follow the guide on how to add and remove sites in MyYoast.
