FAQ

How does the AI Brand Insights scheduling work?

Each brand runs one scheduled analysis every week. You receive email notifications when the analysis is complete.

Can I add more brands?

Yes. Purchase additional subscriptions to add more brands. Each subscription covers one brand and includes one scheduled weekly analysis. 

Which AI tools does the monitoring include?

Currently, ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Perplexity are available, with more AI tools planned for future releases.

Do I need WordPress to use AI Brand Insights?

No. AI Brand Insights is a browser-based tool that runs independently. However, the included WooCommerce SEO plugin requires WordPress and WooCommerce.

What’s your refund policy?

You have one week to request a refund. If more than one AI analysis has been completed during that week, the payment is non-refundable.

Does my plan auto-renew?

Yes. Plans renew annually automatically. Cancel anytime from your account. 

What exactly does Yoast SEO AI+ include?

You get AI Brand Insights monitoring plus everything in the Yoast WooCommerce SEO package, including Yoast AI Generate for image alt text.

Can I use the WooCommerce features without an online store?

The WooCommerce SEO features require a WordPress site with WooCommerce installed. However, AI Brand Insights works independently for any brand monitoring need
