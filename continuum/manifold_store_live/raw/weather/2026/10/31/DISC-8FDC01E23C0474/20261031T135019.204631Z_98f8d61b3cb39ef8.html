About

What is NCEI's mission?

NCEI provides environmental data, products, and services covering the depths of the ocean to the surface of the sun to drive resilience, prosperity, and equity for current and future generations.

What is NCEI's long-term vision?

A tenacious and trusted leader in environmental information for a rapidly changing world with a focus on driving lasting good across our partnerships, our economy, around the U.S and the world.

Why is historical weather data valuable?

NCEI data helps businesses and organizations across sectors operate more efficiently, safely, environmentally, and economically. 

Data Management

The NCEI archive and backup copy contains more than 60 petabytes of data.

Industry and Sectors

NCEI data and products help decision makers address specific challenges and use cases in a variety of societal sectors.

Leadership Team

Deke Arndt

Director

Deke Arndt became NCEI's Director on January 30, 2023 after serving as Chief of NCEI's Climatic Science and Services Division (CSSD) for three years. He previously led the CSSD Monitoring Section. Deke’s career began in 1996 at the Oklahoma Climatological Survey. 

He worked closely with public safety, agriculture, climate monitoring, the energy generation and transmission sectors. In recent years, he served on the Council of the American Meteorological Society, was a co-lead editor of the AMS's annual State of the Climate report, and co-chaired the US Global Change Research Program's Interagency Indicators Working Group.

Joe Pica

Deputy Director

Joe Pica assumed the role of NCEI Deputy Director in 2018 after a three-year stint as Director of the National Weather Service (NWS) Office of Observations, where he oversaw data collection operations to ensure accurate, comprehensive national forecasts. Before joining the NWS, Joe served as Commanding Officer of the NOAA Ships Ronald H. Brown and Okeanos Explorer, respectively the flagship and most technologically advanced vessels in the NOAA fleet. 

Between sea assignments, he brought operational, technical, and leadership skills to a variety of federal assignments including Deputy Director of the former National Geophysical Data Center (NGDC) and Executive Director to the Deputy Under Secretary for Operations. 

Ken Casey

Chief, Data Stewardship Division

Ken Casey began serving as the Chief of the Data Stewardship Division (DSD) on December 29, 2024. Ken graduated from the University of Miami, where he majored in marine science and physics, and went on to earn his PhD in Oceanography at the University of Rhode Island. He joined NCEI in 2001, where he founded and led the Satellite Oceanography group.

Ken has contributed to numerous committees and teams across NOAA, nationally, and internationally, and has served as the President of the Earth Science Information Partners (ESIP), which consists of over 160 members across governmental, academic, and commercial organizations.

Karen Grissom

Chief, Coasts, Oceans, and Geophysics Science Division

Karen Grissom is the acting Division Chief of NCEI’s Coasts, Oceans and Geophysics Science Division. She previously served as both Lead Scientist and operations manager for the NOAA National Data Buoy Center (NDBC), where she helped modernize the Nation’s premier in situ ocean observing system. Before NDBC, Karen deployed emerging technology for the National Ocean Service, and worked as a hydrogeologist for the State of Florida. 

She currently chairs or serves on a number of scientific boards and committees, including the WMO/INFCOM Management Group. She holds a Bachelor of Science in Oceanography from the University of Washington and a Master of Science in Marine Science from the University of Southern Mississippi. 

Scott Hausman

Chief, Mission Support Division

Scott Hausman became Chief of the NCEI Mission Support Division (MSD) in 2017 after two years as Director of NCEI Data Stewardship. Dr. Hausman previously served as Acting Director and Deputy Director of the former National Climatic Data Center (NCDC) and as the Director of the Global Systems Division of the Earth Systems Research Laboratory (ESRL). 

His 24-year career with the U.S. Air Force included command of the 14th Weather Squadron providing climatological support to the Department of Defense, intelligence community, and other federal agencies. 

Jeff Privette

Chief, Climate Science and Services Division

Jeff Privette became Chief of NCEI's Climate Science and Services Division (CSSD) in 2024 following various leadership roles in NCEI and the former National Climatic Data Center (NCDC). He joined NOAA in 2006 to lead its nascent Climate Data Record (CDR) Program, which pioneered the operational production of homogeneous records spanning the satellite era (~1978-present).

Jeff began his career at NASA where he led the MODIS and VIIRS land validation programs and field campaigns, and served as NASA's Deputy Project Scientist for the Suomi-NPP satellite. He currently chairs the Joint CEOS-CGMS Working Group on Climate, among various other service roles.

Aric Whatley

Chief, Resource Management Division

Aric Whatley joined NCEI as chief of the Resource Management Division (RMD) in 2024. He earned a Bachelor of Arts degree in history from North Georgia College, a Master of Arts in International Policy Studies from the Monterey Institute of International Studies, and a Master of Science in National Resource Strategy from the Industrial College of the Armed Forces.

 Aric worked with the Department of Defense for 36 years, both on active duty in the U.S. Army and as a government civilian employee. Aric has held a wide variety of positions throughout DoD, and has years of experience working in resource management.
