Vantage Pro2: Professional Weather Stations for Accurate Data

Engineered for professionals and weather enthusiasts alike, the Davis Vantage Pro2™ is a top-rated, wireless weather station known for its accuracy and versatility. With customizable sensor suites, solar-powered components, and optional UV, solar radiation, and soil moisture sensors, the Vantage Pro2 offers advanced data monitoring for homes, farms, and research. Choose from standalone stations or bundles with the WeatherLink ® Console for expanded insights and connectivity.
