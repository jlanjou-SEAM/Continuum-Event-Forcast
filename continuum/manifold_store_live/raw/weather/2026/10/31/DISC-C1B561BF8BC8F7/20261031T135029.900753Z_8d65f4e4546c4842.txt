## Reference: GALE AI
- [Gale AI API](https://docs.tomorrow.io/reference/gale-ai-api.md): The Gale AI API brings Tomorrow.io's weather intelligence to your application. Ask weather questions in plain language and generate weather summaries- no need to assemble raw data yourself.
  - [Ask a Weather Question](https://docs.tomorrow.io/reference/ask-a-weather-question.md): Ask a free-text weather question and get a natural-language answer, powered by Tomorrow.io's weather intelligence. Supports multi-turn conversations and an optional contextual link.
  - [Weather Summary](https://docs.tomorrow.io/reference/weather-summary.md): Generate a weather summary for a location across a specific time window, powered by Tomorrow.io's weather intelligence.
