Weather Station Sensors: Enhance Your Monitoring

Experience the precision and reliability of Davis Instruments sensors—the gold standard in environmental monitoring technology. Our comprehensive collection delivers professional-grade measurement solutions for weather enthusiasts, agricultural professionals, and environmental researchers alike. 

Each device in our collection represents the pinnacle of sensing technology, from the versatile Anemometer that captures wind data with exceptional accuracy to our specialized Soil Moisture sensor that optimizes irrigation efficiency. Monitor solar radiation levels with our dedicated Solar Radiation Sensor, track air, soil, or water temperature fluctuations with precision using our Stainless Steel Temperature Probes, or measure humidity with confidence using our Temperature/Humidity Sensor. 

The innovative AeroCone Rain Collector ensures precise rainfall measurement even in challenging conditions, while our cutting-edge Sonic Anemometer provides advanced wind measurement without moving parts. Complete your environmental monitoring system with our UV Sensor and Leaf Wetness Sensor for comprehensive data collection. 

Whether you're building a sophisticated network of weather station sensors for residential use, implementing IoT solutions for agriculture to maximize crop yields, or establishing a professional environmental monitoring network, Davis Instruments delivers the accuracy, durability, and reliability you need. Each sensor seamlessly integrates with Davis Vantage Pro2™ weather stations and EnviroMonitor system, providing real-time data you can trust to make informed decisions. 

Elevate your environmental intelligence with Davis Instruments Sensors—where precision meets purpose in every measurement.
